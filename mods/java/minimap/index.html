Mat's Minimap

Mat’s Minimap is a client-side Fabric minimap for Minecraft 26.2, designed to provide a clear and detailed overview of the surrounding terrain without disrupting the vanilla experience.

The map samples every individual block at 0.5x zoom and uses height-based relief shading to make hills, mountains, forests, water, and other terrain easier to distinguish. It automatically switches to a local underground view when exploring caves.

Customizable, lightweight and easy to use.

How to use: Press B to create a waypoint at your current position.
Enter /matsminimap to open the settings menu.

Features

Why Choose Mat's Minimap?

Highly Customizable

Configure map shape, size, zoom, orientation, texture resolution, border style, and player marker.

High-Detail Terrain Mapping

Samples individual blocks to create a detailed overview of forests, rivers, mountains, coastlines, and other terrain.

Powerful Waypoint System

Create, rename, recolor, manage, and delete waypoints. Distant waypoints use compact colored markers to reduce screen clutter.

Useful Entity Tracking

Display nearby mobs, entities, and dropped items using small, unobtrusive map markers.

Multiple Visual Styles

Choose between square and circular maps, Old and Modern borders, or a clean borderless design.

Lightweight and Performance-Focused

Designed for excellent performance with optimized map rendering, efficient terrain caching, and minimal impact on gameplay FPS.

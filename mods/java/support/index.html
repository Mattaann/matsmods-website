Need Help?

Support requests, bug reports and suggestions are handled through GitHub. Open a new issue and describe what happened as clearly as possible.

Alternatively, you can send me the crash report or log file.

Please include the mod name, Minecraft version, mod version and any relevant error messages. Screenshots and log files can also make it easier to understand and solve the problem.

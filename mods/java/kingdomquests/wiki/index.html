Getting Started

The Story

When a new world is created, King Martin's kingdom is generated near world spawn.

The settlement includes a detailed castle, a damaged village, roads, houses, a trading quarter, gardens, a fountain and a fortified outer wall with four gates and corner towers.

You begin as an unknown traveler. Before accepting any quests, you must visit King Martin and listen to his request for help.

Once the introduction is complete, a 42-quest campaign opens across three chapters.

The Kingdom Changes With You

  • The village begins damaged and partially ruined.
  • Houses and roads are gradually restored.
  • New residents and merchants arrive.
  • The settlement becomes fully rebuilt during the later campaign.
  • Completing the story earns recognition as the Hero of the Kingdom.

Kingdom Quests is designed for normal survival gameplay. Story quests must be accepted from their correct NPC, objectives begin tracking after acceptance and rewards are collected from the same quest giver.

Quest System

Quest Journal

Every player receives a Kingdom Quest Journal when joining the world for the first time.

Right-click the journal to open it.

Journal Categories

  • Active Quests
  • Main Quests
  • Adventure Quests
  • Passive Quests
  • Finished Quests
  • Kingdom Progress
  • Chapter introductions
  • Quest giver information
  • Quest Tracker Settings

Lost Your Journal?

Another journal can be obtained using:

/questbook
Quest System

Quest Tracker

Up to three accepted quests can be displayed on the HUD at the same time.

Open an accepted quest in the journal and select:

Track Quest

Tracker Settings

  • Move the tracker
  • Change its size
  • Show or hide it
  • Reset its position
  • Manage up to three tracked quests

Introduction Guidance

Automatic guidance is displayed during the introduction.

  1. Go speak to King Martin.
  2. Go talk to any villager with a ! to get a quest and begin your journey.

These hints do not consume one of the three tracking slots and disappear automatically when their objective is completed.

Passive Progression

Kingdom Challenges

Kingdom Challenges are passive lifetime goals. They do not need to be accepted from an NPC.

Progress is recorded automatically and rewards can be claimed directly from the journal.

  1. Zombie Slayer — Defeat 150 Zombies.
  2. Skeleton Hunter — Defeat 150 Skeletons.
  3. Spider Exterminator — Defeat 100 Spiders.
  4. End Stalker — Defeat 75 Endermen.
  5. Creeper Cleaner — Defeat 100 Creepers.
  6. Master Miner — Mine 2,500 valuable ore blocks.
  7. Lumberjack — Chop 2,000 logs.
  8. Farmer's Pride — Harvest 1,000 crops.
  9. Fisherman — Catch 250 fish with a fishing rod.
  10. Monster Hunter — Defeat 1,000 hostile mobs.
  11. Explorer — Discover 25 different biomes.
  12. Dragon Bane — Defeat 25 hostile Kingdom Drakes.
  13. Treasure Hunter — Open 50 chests.
  14. The Survivor — Remain alive for 100 consecutive Minecraft days.
  15. Completionist — Complete the entire campaign and every other Kingdom Challenge.

Challenge rewards include Emeralds, experience levels, enchanted equipment, enchanted books, a Totem of Undying, Netherite and special chat titles.

Quest Types

Escort and Recovery Quests

When a quest requires the player to find a person or recover an item, the objective is generated on safe terrain somewhere in the world.

The quest giver provides a named compass pointing toward the target.

Escort Quests

  1. Follow the quest compass.
  2. Find the missing person.
  3. Right-click the correct NPC.
  4. Select Follow me.
  5. Lead the person safely back to the kingdom.

Only NPCs connected to an active escort quest display the Follow me option.

Rescued characters become permanent residents and continue living within the royal village.

Recovery Quests

Recovery objectives use uniquely named quest items. Ordinary Bundles or Banners cannot complete these quests accidentally.

Progression

Martin's Forge and MT Points

Martin's Forge is located beside King Martin inside the castle.

MT Points are earned from main quests and Kingdom Challenges. The campaign provides enough points to purchase every permanent attribute upgrade.

Permanent Upgrades

Each category contains ten levels.

Vitality

Increases maximum health.

Swiftness

Increases movement speed.

Strength

Increases attack damage.

Equipment Upgrades

Hold a compatible item in your main hand before opening Martin's Forge.

  • Sharpness up to level V
  • Efficiency up to level V
  • Unbreaking up to level III

The Forge displays the currently held item and the cost before an upgrade is purchased.

World

A Living Kingdom

The settlement changes as the player progresses.

  • Ruined houses are gradually rebuilt.
  • Roads and trading areas are restored.
  • Quest-giver homes contain appropriate workstations.
  • Ordinary villagers live and trade within the settlement.
  • The village includes a fountain, trees, grass, flowers and illuminated roads.
  • The castle contains a throne hall, library, forge, towers, battlements and royal decorations.
  • A fortified outer wall surrounds the entire settlement.
  • Hostile creatures cannot spawn within the walls.
  • Hostile dragons are kept far away from the kingdom.
  • Loose items created during world generation are cleaned automatically.
  • Quest givers are persistent and invulnerable.
  • Villagers remain near their homes and are returned if pathfinding takes them too far away or onto a roof.

Players may build within the kingdom, but King Martin asks them to respect the residents and their homes.

Creatures

Kingdom Drakes

Hostile Kingdom Drakes can be encountered in the wider world.

  • Crimson Kingdom Drake
  • Azure Kingdom Drake
  • Verdant Kingdom Drake

Behaviour

  • Are hostile toward players.
  • Can fly and launch explosive fireballs.
  • Grant substantial experience and valuable loot.
  • Have unique colors and spawn eggs.
  • Cannot spawn near the castle.
  • Count toward the Dragon Bane challenge.

There is no guardian dragon above the castle.

Safety

Inventory Safety

Important rewards and quest items are never silently lost when the player's inventory is full.

The mod fills available inventory slots first. Any remaining items are dropped at the player's feet with a short pickup delay and a warning is displayed.

Protected Items

  • Quest rewards
  • Quest compasses
  • Recovery compasses
  • Starter kits
  • Replacement kits
  • Quest Journals

After dying, players can return to King Martin and request another basic starter kit.

Administration

Admin Menu

The hidden admin menu is opened with:

/matskingdom

The command requires Game Master or operator permission. On a multiplayer server, the player must be OP or have equivalent command permissions.

Admin Features

  • Display every quest
  • Navigate between quest pages
  • Complete any quest without accepting it
  • Grant the quest's normal rewards
  • Grant the quest's MT Points
  • Add 10 MT Points
  • Add 50 MT Points
  • Add 100 MT Points
  • Remain on the currently selected page after an action

Every admin action is validated by the server. A modified client cannot use the menu without the required permissions.

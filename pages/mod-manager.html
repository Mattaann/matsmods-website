Mat's Mod Manager

Want an easier way to manage your Minecraft mods? With Mat's Mod Manager, you can quickly enable or disable your installed mods without editing files or restarting your workflow.

It also includes several built-in quality-of-life features:

  • No Creeper Explosions
  • Tree Felling
  • Enchantment Descriptions
  • Block Identification Overlay
Choose exactly which features you want to use and customize your Minecraft experience your way.

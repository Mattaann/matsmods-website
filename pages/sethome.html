Mat's Set home

With Mat's Set Home, you can save important locations anywhere in your Minecraft world and quickly teleport back to them whenever you need to.

Simply stand where you want your home to be and use:

/sethome

 The mod will remember the exact coordinates of that location. You can then return there at any time by using:

/home

Download Back to Mods

How It Works

  1. Stand where you want to save your home.
  2. Type /sethome to save your location.
  3. Use /home anytime to teleport back.
  4. Create additional homes with /sethome2 and /sethome3.
  5. Open /matssethome to manage or remove saved homes.

Mat's Set Home

With Mat's Set Home, you can save important locations anywhere in your Minecraft world and quickly teleport back whenever you need to.

Create up to three homes, return to them using simple commands and manage everything through an easy in-game menu.

Lightweight, simple and designed to feel like a natural part of vanilla Minecraft.

Back to Mods

How It Works

  1. Stand where you want to save your home.
  2. Type /sethome to save your first location.
  3. Use /home anytime to teleport back.
  4. Create additional homes with /sethome2 and /sethome3.
  5. Teleport to them with /home 2 and /home 3.
  6. Open /matssethome to view, manage or remove saved homes.

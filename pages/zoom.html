Mat's Zoom

Tired of not being able to see what is in the distance? With Mat's Zoom, you can easily zoom from 1× up to 100× and get a closer look without leaving Minecraft.

Adjust the zoom level with a simple slider, customize the controls and choose your preferred mouse sensitivity while zoomed in.

Customizable, lightweight and easy to use.

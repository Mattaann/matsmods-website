Mat's Zoom

Tired of not being able to see what is in the distance?

With Mat's Zoom, you can smoothly zoom from 1× all the way up to 100× without leaving Minecraft.

Easily adjust your zoom level with a slider, customize your keybinds and choose your preferred mouse sensitivity while zoomed in.

Lightweight, highly customizable and built to feel like a natural part of vanilla Minecraft.

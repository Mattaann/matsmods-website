Mat's Damage Indicator

Enhances combat by giving you clear visual feedback while keeping Minecraft's vanilla feel. Whenever you hit an entity, the damage dealt is displayed near your crosshair, making it easy to see how effective your attacks are.

Choose exactly which features you want to use and customize your Minecraft experience your way.

Looking at a mob also shows a clean target HUD with its name, 3D model, and current health, helping you track enemies during combat.

How to use: Type /matsdmgind to open menu.
